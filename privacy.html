privacy policy

LamTekk TrendEngine ("TrendEngine") is a personal app that runs only on its owner's computer. This page explains what it does with data from the accounts its owner connects.

youtube api services

TrendEngine uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube account you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

what it accesses

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos the owner has approved, to the owner's own channel.
  • youtube.readonly: to read the channel name (to show which account is connected) and the public view, like and comment counts of videos TrendEngine uploaded.

where it's kept

  • Sign-in tokens are stored on the owner's computer, in files only the owner's user account can read.
  • The channel name, video ids and the counts above are stored in a database on the same computer.
  • Nothing is sent to any TrendEngine server (there isn't one), sold, shared, or used for advertising.
  • Data from YouTube is only sent back to YouTube, to make the requests above.

how long it's kept, and how to remove it

  • Counts are fetched for a limited window after each upload (30 days by default) and kept until the owner deletes them.
  • Pressing "disconnect" in TrendEngine deletes the stored tokens and asks Google to cancel them.
  • You can also remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
