LamTekk TrendEngine

TrendEngine is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It collects public news and trend headlines, helps write research notes and drafts, and, after the owner approves each one, posts them to the owner's own social accounts.

what it does with your accounts

  • youtube: uploads short videos the owner has approved to the owner's channel, and reads view, like and comment counts for those videos.
  • nothing is posted without the owner's approval of that exact version.
  • sign-in details are kept on the owner's computer only. There is no TrendEngine server and no account to create.

who it's for

It's built and used by one person for their own channels. It isn't offered to the public.
